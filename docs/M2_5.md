# M2.5 "Shop, service and street"

Plan approved 22 Nov 2026 (after the M2 review). Steps S1–S9; progress at the end.

## Context

M2 passed its perf gate, but in your review it doesn't play like a tea shop yet:
- Customers walk anywhere that isn't furniture, including the dirt filler and the road. The shop has only two cutaway back walls, so the east side and the whole front are open.
- Nobody is in the shop: cups are poured from a bubble tap or auto-served after 45 s.
- The start is an empty room plus a big inventory of mixed furniture.
- Debug UI shows up in screenshots.

M2.5 adds:
- real walkable zones and a shop with walls, a front and a back kitchen
- a player character who does the service by hand (a calm Diner Dash)
- a task board shared with a first staff member, the waiter
- a small, deliberate starting shop with readable sprites and shadows
- debug UI hidden unless `?debug=1`

All CLAUDE.md hot-path and performance rules still apply.

**Naming:** "M2.5" is already the name of an M2 step (customer sprites, in docs/M2.md). The new milestone gets its own doc, `docs/M2_5.md`, and its steps are numbered **S1–S9** so nothing gets mixed up.

**Order of work:** if you approve this plan, S1 (doc updates only) comes first and is committed on its own. Then the build steps follow. If you'd rather approve the doc text before any code, say so and I'll stop after S1.

## Decisions I'm proposing (change any of these when you approve)

1. **Crossing the road.** People never walk on the road. Customers arrive at both ends of the near pavement (the shop side). They also come from the far pavement across one zebra crossing near the shop. The crossing is the only road tile people may use. Vehicles come later (M4); the road is just blocked for people until then.
2. **No auto-serve.** Every cup is made by a person, either the player or staff. Customers wait as long as it takes and never leave unhappy.
   - Waiting longer only lowers the tip: tip = pour-quality tip × a wait factor. The wait factor runs from 1.25 (fast) down to 0, never below 0. The price is always paid in full.
   - At 17:00 no new customers come, and the evening starts once everyone seated has been served and has left. The clock pauses while the tab is hidden, so this can't stall in the background.
3. **Cups are a fixed set** (`shop.json`, 10 to start). Each cup is on the shelf (clean), on the tray, on a table, or at the sink (dirty).
   - With no clean cups, brewing waits until someone washes. No failure, just slower service.
   - Cups reset to clean on load (customers aren't saved mid-day either).
4. **The pour minigame runs for every cup the player brews.** When the player reaches the counter, the existing pour overlay opens. The tray holds 2 cups. Brew picks the oldest order that hasn't been brewed yet.
5. **Staff vs player.** A task the player queues is the player's. If a staff member had already claimed it, they drop it and choose again.
6. **Waiter.**
   - Unlocks at savings ≥ 60,000 kyat and ≥ 6 seats. Wage 3,000 kyat a day, paid at Sleep.
   - If the tin holds less than the wage, the waiter takes an unpaid day off tomorrow and nothing is taken from the tin.
   - Hiring and letting go happen in the evening panel.
   - All of these numbers live in `src/data/staff.json`.
7. **Starting shop (new games).**
   - Placed: tea counter, kettle, sink, stove, 3 low tables, 8 low stools, 2 plants.
   - Starting inventory is empty. The M3 furniture shop sells more.
   - The keys `stool`/`table` stay (saves store keys) and get redrawn as low furniture. Chair, bench and the rest stay in the catalog for M3.
   - The signboard shows a default shop name; naming it stays in M5.
8. **Default zoom.** Characters should be about 40 CSS px tall on a landscape phone. The default view becomes about 200 world px high instead of 270 (one whole step in). 270 stays as the most zoomed-out view.
   - This changes the CLAUDE.md pixel-art line, so S1 updates CLAUDE.md as well.
9. **Older saves.** Save v3 is a new parser plus a 2→3 migration.
   - It adds any missing fixtures (counter, kettle, sink, stove).
   - Furniture now standing on a wall line, in the kitchen or outside the allowed zones goes back to the inventory. It is never deleted.

## S2 — Walkable zones and walls

**Zone layer.** `shared/layout.ts` gains `ZONE` bits: `road`, `crossing`, `pavement`, `front` (opening and door tiles), `interior`, `kitchen`, `blocked`. They join the existing `owned`/`roof` bits; the zone grid is u8, so it needs 8 bits in total, otherwise a separate `access` u8 grid.

**Access rules.** New `shared/rules/access.ts` holds the access classes as data: `people` (customers) and `staff` (player and staff).
- people: pavement, crossing, front, interior
- staff: pavement, crossing, front, interior, kitchen
- road and filler: nobody

**Walls.** `WALL` gets real wall edges for walking. `n`/`w` edges already block steps. The front (+y side) and east side (+x side) are stored as the n edge of the tile beyond them and the w edge of the tile beyond them, so no new bits are needed.
- The front edge is open across the opening.
- The kitchen partition has one door tile.
- Rendering stays a cutaway: front and side walls draw as a low sill only.

**Walk grid.** `sim/path/walk-grid.ts` gets one cost array per access class (people, staff): two `Uint8Array`s plus two move masks, so 2× memory (~12 KB). The dirty-rect update is unchanged.
- `PathFinder.request(from, to, cls)` picks the class arrays; the A* core is unchanged.
- Customers use `people`, workers use `staff`.

**Area.** `sim/area.ts` builds:
- the shop interior, a back kitchen strip with stove and sink space, the counter line at the front, and the opening
- pavements, road and a zebra crossing
- filler tiles marked `blocked`, with neighbouring building facades (the render part is in S3)

Customer spawn and exit points move to the pavement ends and the far side of the crossing (`STREET` → a `SPAWNS` list).

**Placement.** `shared/rules/placement.ts`:
- Customer seats and furniture: interior only.
- Kitchen fixtures: kitchen only.
- Nothing may go on an entrance tile, or block the only route from the entrance to the counter. The reachability check reuses the walk flood fill.

**Tests** (new `test/zones.test.ts`):
- From every spawn point to every seat in the starting layout and in random furnished layouts, every path tile is in an allowed zone. The path enters the interior only through a `front` tile and never crosses a wall edge.
- The same check for exits.
- A staff path reaches the sink through the kitchen door; a customer path never enters the kitchen.
- Old saves: furniture on now-illegal tiles goes back to the inventory (migration test).

## S4 — Task board, cups and the new customer flow (sim only)

**Task board.** New `sim/service/task-board.ts`, a struct of arrays with a fixed capacity of 128 and handles:
- kind: order, brew, serve, clear, wash, cook
- target: customer handle, cup id, or fixture tile
- state: open or claimed
- claimedBy: worker index
- created tick, plus a counter for each kind

`cook` exists but nothing creates it until snacks (M5).

**Cups.** New `sim/service/cups.ts`: a fixed pool, each with a place (shelf, tray, table, sink), a drink and a tile.

**Customer states** (`sim/npc/customers.ts`):
- The new order: toSeat → wantsOrder (the order task appears and the bubble shows "?") → ordered (shows the drink; a brew task appears) → served → drinking → leaving, and the cup stays behind (a clear task appears).
- Removed: the auto-serve timer and `pourHold`.
- Payment happens at serve, with the wait factor (`shared/drinks.ts` `tipFor(drink, score, waitTicks)`, tested).
- Closing: the evening needs no customers in the shop.

**Tests:**
- task lifecycle
- cups conserved (always N cups in total)
- wait-factor tips
- a full day with a headless **bot player** (greedy over the board). It serves everyone, money only rises, no task stays open after the evening, and it's deterministic over 3 days. This replaces `test/m2-gate.test.ts` and the auto-serve tests in `test/customers.test.ts`.
- The arrival curve in `shop.json` is tuned so the solo bot's average wait stays reasonable (target ≤ 60 s of game time, recorded in M2_5.md).

## S5 — The player character (sim + protocol v9)

**Workers.** New `sim/npc/workers.ts`: a struct of arrays for the player and staff, with position, path, tray (2 cup ids) and state.
- They walk with the staff access class.
- They do a task with an action time from `shop.json` (take order 1.5 s, clear 1 s, wash 2 s per cup).

**Player queue.** Up to 3 task handles. It's part of `PlayerState`, so the UI shows the markers.

**Actions:**
- `queueTask {task}`: refused when the queue is full or the task is gone, with i18n reasons.
- `cancelTask {task}`
- Brew: when the player reaches the counter, the worker sends a `brewReady` event. The UI opens the pour overlay and the existing `serve` action fills the cup onto the tray. Cancelling puts the brew task back on the board.

**Frames.** Workers use the existing NPC_SPAWN/STATE sections with new def ids. New sections, written only when something changed, with no allocation:
- TASK (kind 10): u32 handle | u8 kind | u8 queuePos | u16 pad | i16 x | i16 y
- CUP (kind 11): u16 id | u8 place | u8 drink | i16 x | i16 y

**Tests:** protocol and frame round-trips, plus queue rules:
- max 3
- cancel
- a staff claim taken over by the player

## S6 — Render and UI for service

**Render:**
- `render/npcs.ts` draws the player and waiter sprites (from `tools/placeholder-characters.ts`) and the cups on the tray.
- New `render/service.ts` draws cups on tables, dirty cups at the sink, and the steam on the kettle.

**Tap handling** in `input/` + `game/`:
- Main resolves a tap to the task under it, by priority: customer bubble, table cup, counter, sink.
- It sends `queueTask`, or `cancelTask` if the tap was on a queued marker.
- Numbered markers 1–3 are DOM, in `ui/hud/queue.ts`, placed once per change rather than every frame (the same pattern as the reaction bubbles).

**Pour overlay** (`ui/pour/`) now opens from `brewReady` instead of a bubble tap.

**New Myanmar strings**, listed for your end-of-project review: order "?", queue full, nothing to wash, and the waiter / hire / wage / day-off texts.

## S7 — The waiter (staff)

**Choosing tasks.** `sim/npc/staff.ts` uses utility scoring on the same board:
- score = role weight[kind] − distance × k + age × k + jitter (`npcDecide`)
- the waiter's kinds are order, serve and clear
- it re-scores when it's idle, never per tick for everyone

**Hiring.** `src/data/staff.json` holds the roles (all four defined; only the waiter can be hired), unlock rules and wages.
- Actions `hire {role}` and `dismiss {role}` work in the evening only.
- At Sleep, wages come out of the tin. If the tin is too low, it's a day off: the waiter is absent tomorrow and no money moves. Events report both.

**Save v3:** the staff roster (role key, hired day, day-off flag) plus the S2 migration. It's a new parser plus a migration; older parsers stay untouched.

**UI:** the evening panel gets a hire row (locked / hire / hired + wage / day off).

**Tests:**
- unlock rules
- wages never push money below 0
- a day off on a low tin
- a waiter + bot day serves faster than the bot alone
- save v2→v3 round-trip

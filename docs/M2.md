# M2 — Shop day

## Context

M0 and M1 are done and deployed: you can build a shop, and saves work. M2 makes the shop run.
**Done when** (DESIGN §11): customers arrive, choose seats by utility, pour minigame, savings tin, day end and teaser.

Decisions made with the user (21 Nov 2026):
- **Day shape:** shutters open at 06:00 and the shop serves until 17:00 (~11 real min at 1 game h = 1 real min). After 17:00 no new customers come, and the last ones finish. The clock then **holds in the evening until the player taps Sleep**, which skips to the next 06:00. The night is never played. The clock also holds at 06:00 until the player taps **Open shutters**, which gives the day a natural start.
- **Serving:** the player pours drinks in the minigame. If they don't, there is an **auto-serve fallback**: after a delay the customer gets a basic cup at a lower price and no tip, so nobody leaves unserved. A poured cup earns the full price plus a tip based on how well it matches the order.
- **Pathfinding:** the grid A* core is built now in `sim/path/`. Customers use it in M2, and tap-to-walk reuses it in M3.
- **Ingredients:** unlimited and free in M2. Stock and costs come with the M3 wet market.

## Steps (commit after each; `npm run check` before every commit)

### M2.1 Data + shared drink model
- `src/data/shop.json`: open/close minutes, arrival rate per hour (morning rush), patience, auto-serve delay and price factor, drink time, tip curve, seat-scoring weights, tin sizes. All balancing numbers live here.
- `src/data/drinks.json`: the classic presets (pon man, cho seint, kya seint; "plain" was dropped on 22 Nov 2026) with price and target pour (tea/milk fractions → strong, sweet, creamy).
- `src/data/customers.json`: generic customer looks and taste ranges (no named regulars until M5).
- `src/shared/drinks.ts` (pure): pour amounts → taste point → satisfaction 0–1 → price + tip. Both the worker (authority) and the minigame (live hint) use it.
- `src/shared/day.ts`: phase from game minute (closed-morning / open / closing / evening).
- Tests: satisfaction curve, preset targets score 1, phase boundaries.

### M2.2 Day cycle in the sim + save v2
- `Sim` gets a day phase. The clock holds at 06:00 until `{k:'openShop'}`, runs through 17:00, then holds in the evening once the shop is empty. `{k:'sleep'}` builds the day summary and teaser, moves to the next 06:00 and saves.
- Day stats (served, poured, auto-served, earned, best satisfaction) are tracked in the worker.
- **Save v2**: phase, today's stats, and tomorrow's teaser effect. Add `PARSERS[2]` and `MIGRATIONS[1]` (v1 → v2 defaults); don't edit the v1 parser. Customers are not saved: they are transient, so a mid-day load reopens with an empty shop, and the money already earned is kept.
- Protocol v5: new actions `openShop`, `sleep`, `startPour`, `cancelPour`, `serve {id, tea, milk}`. New `SimEvent`s: `money`, `served`, `dayEnd {stats}`, `teaser {key, args}`. All text is sent as keys.
- Tests: the clock holds and resumes, sleep advances exactly one day, save v1 → v2 migration, round trip.

### M2.3 A* pathfinding core (`sim/path/`)
- `walkCost` Uint8 grid derived from floor and occupancy, updated only in the dirty rect after build changes. Furniture blocks. Seat tiles are reachable only as a destination. Back walls block the edge they sit on (`WALL.n`/`WALL.w`).
- A* with a typed-array binary heap, 4-way or 8-way with no corner cutting, and a node-expansion budget per tick (≤ 2 ms, continued next tick). Results go into the `pathPool` (Uint16 tile indices, offset/len, free list). Zero allocation on the hot path.
- Paths in flight are checked after build changes; a blocked customer re-paths.
- Tests: around obstacles, through the door edge only, unreachable target, budget split across ticks gives the same result, no allocation in steady state (pool reuse).

### M2.4 Customer simulation (`sim/npc/`)
- NPC SoA per ARCHITECTURE §3 (x, y, state, stateT, def, taste, seat, path, pathStep, dir, anim, gen), capacity 256, handles from `shared/ids.ts`.
- Arrivals: hourly rate curve × shop appeal (seat count, average beauty/comfort), drawn from the `npcDecide` stream. Spawn at a street edge.
- **Seat choice by utility:** score every free seat tile (`seat` count in objects.json; a bench gives 2) with the property grids' `value` arrays (comfort, noise vs the customer's quiet preference, light, beauty, shade/warmth) + table next to it + distance + small jitter. The best seat wins and is reserved. If there is no free seat, the customer walks on by.
- States: arrive → walk to seat → sit + order (bubble) → wait (the `startPour` reservation pauses auto-serve) → served (poured, or auto after the delay) → drink → pay into the tin → leave → despawn off-street.
- **Build while open:** if a used seat is moved, picked up or sold, its customer stands and chooses again (or leaves if they had already paid). Placement ignores NPCs; blocked walkers re-path.
- Frames: NPC_SPAWN / NPC_STATE (changed only) / NPC_DESPAWN are already in `diff.ts`. Add `NPC_ORDER` (kind 9: u32 handle | u8 drink | u8 orderState) for bubbles.
- Tests: deterministic seat choice for a seed, a quieter seat is preferred by a quiet-taste customer, a headless full day (money rises, every NPC despawns by evening, nobody gets stuck), removing an occupied seat, the auto-serve path.

### M2.5 Rendering customers
- Placeholder characters (~16×24, ≤ 32-colour palette, a few looks, 4 directions × 2 walk frames + sitting) generated by `tools/make-placeholders.ts` and repacked into the atlas (`npm run atlas`). Stay within 2 × 1024² atlases.
- `game/view.ts`: NPC mirror (prev/cur x, y, dir, anim, look, order) from the frame handler.
- `render/npcs.ts`: pooled sprites, interpolated between the last two frames, snapped to whole pixels, sorted in the **same sortable container as objects** (a seated customer gets seat depth + 1). Animated NPCs are capped by tier (12 / 25 / 40); the rest are frozen. Order bubble sprite above the head; pixel-accurate tap picking hands the customer to the pour UI.
- If tall multi-tile objects draw wrongly next to characters, slice those sprites (known M1 limit).

### M2.6 Pour minigame (`ui/pour/`)
- DOM overlay that opens when you tap an order bubble: the order's name and a pixel cup (CSS layers, so no new runtime dependency). **Hold** to pour tea, then **hold** to pour condensed milk, with a soft target mark for the ordered preset. Release ends a pour. There is no timer, and Cancel is always available.
- Pointer Events + Space on desktop; touch targets ≥ 44 px; safe-area padding.
- It sends `startPour` when it opens and `serve {id, tea, milk}` when you finish. The worker scores it with `shared/drinks.ts` and replies with the payment and a reaction key, shown as a small bubble.

### M2.7 Savings tin + day HUD
- `ui/hud/tin.ts`: the tin's fill level is the money display (tin size steps from shop.json), with kyat in Myanmar digits and a coin-drop animation on `money` events. It replaces the plain money readout outside build mode.
- Clock (hour) readout; an **Open shutters** button in the morning; HUD on the left/right edges with `env(safe-area-inset-*)`.
- Building stays available all day.

### M2.8 Day end + teaser
- Evening panel once the last customer leaves: served count, poured vs auto, earnings, the best reaction. A **Sleep** button saves and shows a sleep screen with **one teaser line**.
- `src/data/teasers.json`: teaser kinds whose promise **really happens tomorrow** (stored in save v2): "a customer promised to bring friends" → extra morning arrivals; "someone asked about sweet tea" → a cho seint lover arrives; and so on. The choice comes from the day's stats + the `events` stream. Calm rules: teasers are only good news.
- Music follows the time of day (morning → afternoon → night on the sleep screen) through the existing music policy.

### M2.9 Gate + docs
- Headless Vitest: 3 full seeded days give identical results across runs (determinism), with no stuck NPCs and money only going up.
- Perf gate (`tools/dev/gate.mjs`): production build, 150-object shop + a full room of customers at peak, 6× throttle Low and 4× Mid, within Low budgets (≤ 25 ms frame, ≤ 40 draws, texture ≤ 48 MB, heap ≤ 100 MB). Worker tick time on the overlay. Pathfinding ≤ 2 ms per tick.
- Browser checks via `tools/dev/probe.mjs`: open → customers sit → pour → tin rises → evening → sleep → teaser → reload keeps the day.
- Docs: `docs/M2.md` (gate table), ARCHITECTURE (protocol v5, save v2, NPC_ORDER, paths), STATUS.md, new `my.json` keys flagged for your Burmese review, BACKLOG for anything deferred.

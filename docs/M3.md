# M3 "Neighbourhood"

Approved 24 Nov 2026. Steps S1–S9; progress at the end.

## Context

After M2.5 the shop plays like a tea shop, but the world is one street of decorative fronts, ingredients are free and unlimited, and furniture only comes from the start stock. M3 (DESIGN.md §11): 3 streets reusing the M2.5 zones, walking the neighbourhood, 4 shops with daily stock, carrying and delivery — plus floor/wall painting (deferred from M1 to the paint stall) and the first use of `rng.market`.

## Decisions (approved 24 Nov 2026)

1. **One bigger map, not separate scenes.** The area grows from 64×48 to 128×96 (`MAX_AREA_*`). The shop keeps its exact tile coordinates, so saves need no object migration. The tea street stays; a market street and a quiet lane join it. The shop keeps running while you walk (same sim). Floor chunks are already pooled (≤ 48 live). Every errand is ≤ 40 s of walking (≤ ~130 path tiles at 3.5 tiles/s); a test checks it.
2. **Four shops** (data in `src/data/neighbourhood.json`):

   | Shop | Street | Sells | Hours |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | Wet market | market street | tea leaves, condensed milk, evaporated milk | 05:00–11:00 |
   | Furniture shop | the lane | stools, chairs, benches, tables, shelves, rugs, plants | 08:00–20:00 |
   | Hardware & paint stall | the lane | floor tiles and wall paint, by the tile | 08:00–20:00 |
   | Bookstall | market street | decor (book stack, newspaper rack, poster stand, radio) | 07:00–19:00 |

   The clock already waits in the morning (before Open) and the evening (before Sleep), so errands fit before opening and after closing without leaving customers waiting.
3. **Daily stock.** Each morning `rng.market` reseeds from (save seed, day) and picks each shop's items, quantities and a price factor 0.85–1.15. Deterministic, so a reload shows the same stock; only today's purchases are saved. Sold-out items return another day.
4. **Ingredients are real; service never stalls.** Each drink lists what it uses (tea leaves plus condensed and/or evaporated milk, ~250 kyat per 1,000-kyat cup). A new game starts with ~2 days of stock. Ingredients are reserved when the order is taken; if the customer's drink can't be made they pick one that can; if nothing can be made they get free ရေနွေးကြမ်း and leave content, paying nothing. A HUD shelf shows cups left; the evening teaser warns when stock is low.
5. **Walking.** Outside build mode, a tap on empty walkable ground walks the player there (WASD on desktop); a tap on a shopkeeper or shop front queues a **visit**. Both use the 3-slot queue and markers as player-only task kinds `go` and `visit`. The camera follows the player outside the shop; a home button re-centres on the shop.
6. **Carrying and delivery.** The player carries up to 4 load (ingredient pack = 1, furniture = w×h). Goods are put away as soon as the player steps into the shop. Delivery costs 10 % (min 500 kyat) and arrives 2 game hours later (bought in the evening → next opening). Carried goods and pending deliveries are saved.
7. **Painting.** Floor tiles and wall paint are bought by the tile and kept as materials. Build mode gets a paint tool (tap or drag owned tiles; 1 unit per tile; undo refunds). The street can't be painted. 4 floor finishes + 4 wall colours to start.
8. **Neighbourhood map.** A small DOM overlay: a 1-px-per-tile canvas drawn once from the zone grid, shops as ≥ 44 px buttons, the player's dot. Tapping a shop queues the visit.
9. **Save v4** adds ingredient stock, materials, painted floor/wall overrides (owned tiles only), carried goods, pending deliveries and today's purchases. New parser + v3→v4 migration that gives old saves a new game's starting ingredients.
10. **Not in M3:** shop owners remembering you, phone orders, bicycle; crafting/workbench; wall-mounted items; residential properties and the notice board (M4); NPC shoppers (M4); real shop interiors (shops are fronts with a counter and keeper).

## Steps

### S1 — Docs
DESIGN.md (§2, §4, §5, §8, §10, §11, §13), ARCHITECTURE.md (protocol v10, neighbourhood data and area builder, market sim, save v4), this file, STATUS, BACKLOG.

### S2 — The neighbourhood map (data + sim)
- `src/data/neighbourhood.json`: streets (pavement/road/crossing rects), building fronts, the four shops (front rect, keeper tile, visit tile, hours, stock pool), customer entry points.
- `shared/neighbourhood.ts` validates it (the `buildStaff` pattern); `shared/street.ts` keeps `SHOP`/`KITCHEN`/`OPENING`… with the same values.
- `sim/area.ts` builds 128×96 grids generically: floors, `WZ` zones, facades, sills. Shop counters are blocked; the visit tile is pavement.
- Customers enter from data entry points near the tea street.
- Tests: zones over the whole map (every entry → every seat, crossings only); every visit tile within 130 path tiles of the shop door; old saves load with every object in place; long paths spread across ticks within 700 nodes/tick.

### S3 — Street art and camera
- Placeholder art: market stalls, furniture shop, hardware stall, bookstall, 4 keepers, junction/lane floors, more facade variants; palette-checked, baked shadows; atlas still one 1024² page.
- Shop fronts as sorted sprites from the data; keepers as static NPC sprites.
- Camera follow (dead zone, whole pixels, no allocation) and a home button.
- Perf spot check: walk across the map at 6× (chunk churn, texture memory).

### S4 — Walking and visits (protocol v10)
- Player-only task kinds `go` (tile) and `visit` (shop); `walkTo` / `interact` add them to the queue. Arrival sends `atShop {shop}`; the UI opens the shop panel.
- WASD sends `walkTo` for the next tile.
- Tests: mixed service/errand queues, visit arrival, staff never claim `go`/`visit`, determinism.

### S5 — Ingredients and the no-stall rule
- `drinks.json` `uses`; `src/data/ingredients.json` (key, pack size in cups, base price); start stock in `start.json`.
- `sim/service/stock.ts`: counts, reserve at `orderTaken`, release on cancel. Drink choice filtered by what can be made; free-tea fallback.
- PlayerState `stock`; HUD shelf; teaser `teaser.lowStock`.
- Tests: stock never negative; a zero-stock day ends cleanly; reservations conserved; bot day deterministic.

### S6 — Shops and daily stock
- `sim/market/market.ts`: daily stock from `rng.market` reseeded by (seed, day); `buy(shop, item, qty, deliver)` checks hours, money, stock, and that the player stands at the visit tile.
- `ui/shop/panel.ts`: rows with icon, price, stock; buy 1 / buy all; carry or deliver; load shown; closes when walking away. Stock via a `marketStock` request/ack.
- Tests: same seed + day → same stock; money never negative; refused when closed or away; sold-out returns.

### S7 — Carrying and delivery
- Carry slots (type + qty, load ≤ 4), put away on entering interior/kitchen/front. Deliveries: (due minute, type, qty), arrive on the tick or at the next opening. Carry sprite.
- Save v4 (stock, carry, deliveries, purchases, materials, paint).
- Tests: load cap; put away; delivery timing incl. evening → morning; v4 round-trip; v3 → v4 migration.

### S8 — Floor and wall painting
- Materials; build-mode paint tool with ghost tint; `paint {x, y, layer, finish}` builder command with undo/redo; owned tiles only (rule in `shared/rules/placement.ts`).
- Wall colour grid next to `wall`; TILE_PATCH streams it; chunks pick wall texture by colour. Saves store finish keys.
- Tests: owned only; materials; undo refunds; save round-trip.

### S9 — Gate and docs
- Browser: `tools/dev/errand.js` (market → buy → carry home → stock rises; furniture by delivery arrives), `paint.js`, map, `service.js` / `reload.mjs` still pass.
- Perf gate (crowd 40) Low 6× and Mid 4×, plus a walk-across mode with camera follow; re-run the M2.5 fps columns on an idle machine.
- Headless: zones, errands, stock, 3-day determinism with market trips.
- Gate table, ARCHITECTURE, STATUS (M4 next), push, live check.

## Protocol v10 (summary)

- Actions: `walkTo {x,y}`, `interact {id}` (visit), `buy {shop, item, qty, deliver}`, `paint {x, y, layer, finish}`, `marketStock {shop}` (ack returns rows).
- Events: `atShop`, `delivered`, `putAway`, `freeTea`.
- PlayerState: `stock`, `materials`, `carry`, `deliveries`.
- TASK section: kinds `go` = 6, `visit` = 7; layout unchanged.

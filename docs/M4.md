# M4 "Living world": plan

## Context

M3 is done and deployed. The neighbourhood is one 112×72 map with four shops, errands, real ingredients and painting. It still feels empty:
- Only customers and staff move.
- Shopkeepers are static sprites, and the road has no traffic.
- Buildings are decoration only.
- Being away does nothing: `worker.ts` posts empty catch-up progress.

DESIGN.md §11 M4 lists:
- schedules (including staff)
- levels of detail (LOD)
- the property market
- gossip
- offline catch-up (including staff and wages)
- vehicles on the road
- the helper role

STATUS adds hooks from M3:
- `WZ.road` is free for vehicles.
- `entry` buildings are where residents live.
- Catch-up must use up stock and deliver every pending delivery.
- Shoppers at the market stalls.
- The BACKLOG note on customers walking single file (separation).

Intended outcome: the street has its own life without you. Neighbours you recognise walk to work, the market and your shop. Traffic stops at the crossings. Buildings come up for sale and change hands. You hear about it from your customers and the notice board, and you can buy or rent the space next door to grow the shop. Coming back after days away shows what happened while you were gone. Everything stays calm.

The milestone doc is `docs/M4.md`; its steps are numbered S1–S11. Protocol goes to v11 and the save to v5.

## Decisions (approved 25 Nov 2026)

1. **Residents.** 24 named neighbours, defined in `src/data/residents.json`.
   - **Data per resident:** name key, home (an `entry` building door), look, job, tea habits (time window, drink, how often) and a daily schedule template.
   - **Jobs:**
     - the 4 shopkeepers (they walk to their stall before opening and home after closing)
     - "works off the map" (leaves by a street end in the morning and comes back in the evening)
     - at home
   - **Schedule blocks** are per hour: home, work, market, tea shop, stroll.
   - **Residents become customers.** A resident going to your tea shop goes through the existing customer pipeline (seat choice, order, pay). They keep their name key and count visits, which feeds friendship in M5.
   - **Generic customers stay for volume.** Resident visits replace part of the generic arrival rate, so daily income barely changes.
   - **Market shoppers.** Residents with a market block walk to free pavement spots in front of a stall and stand there for 5–15 game minutes.
2. **LOD: the camera never changes the outcome.**
   - All movement and decisions run the same everywhere, which keeps tests and catch-up deterministic.
   - Schedules decide on the hour; walkers follow real paths. This is cheap: about 40 walkers and roughly one path request every few seconds.
   - Paths between common place pairs (door → shop front, door → stall) go into a cache that is cleared when the walk grid changes.
   - **Near / Far** only affects streaming. The main thread sends `viewport {x0,y0,x1,y1}` (the ARCHITECTURE message reserved for M4).
     - The worker sends NPC_SPAWN/STATE only for NPCs inside it plus a 4-tile margin, and NPC_DESPAWN when they leave.
     - Far NPCs therefore cost the main thread nothing.
     - The animated-NPC tier cap (12/25/40) stays in render.
   - **Abstract** is catch-up (decision 7).
3. **Staff schedules and the helper.**
   - Staff arrive on foot from a street end during the morning and go home in the evening. A staff member who arrives after Open just starts late.
   - The helper (wash and clear, 2,000 kyat a day, unlocks at 150,000 savings and 10 seats; these numbers are already in `staff.json`) becomes hireable.
   - Wages and the unpaid-day-off rule are unchanged.
4. **Property market.**
   - **Data:** each building and lot in `src/data/properties.json` has an owner (a resident, or an off-map landlord), a value and a kind (house, small business, lot).
   - **States:** Not selling → Considering → Listed (sale or rent) → Sold / back to Not selling. The rolls use the `events` stream once a day at Sleep, with the DESIGN triggers:
     - a life event: Considering, then Listed after 1–3 weeks
     - a business closing: 30 % a week after a slow spell
     - an urgent sale: 15–25 % below value, short expiry
     - listings expire after 7–21 days, and NPC buyers may buy first
   - The four market shops never close: you need them, and that keeps it calm.
   - **On the street:**
     - A listed front shows a small "for sale / for rent" sign.
     - A closed business shows shutters down.
     - When a house sells, its resident may move away and a new resident (from a spare pool in the data) moves in.
   - **Rent:**
     - Rent is optional and only applies to a space you chose to rent.
     - It's paid at Sleep like wages.
     - If the tin can't cover it, the lease ends calmly. Furniture in that space goes back to your inventory, there is never debt, and the space comes back on the market later.
5. **Growing the shop.**
   - Two spaces join your shop:
     - **East lot:** the one-storey front at x 37–41. Listed on day 3 (a gift: first listings come early). About 250,000 kyat to buy or 3,000 a day to rent.
     - **West building:** x 17–24. Listed later through a life event. About 600,000 to buy or 6,000 a day to rent.
   - Buying or renting rebuilds that part of the area:
     - interior floor
     - a low sill on the pavement side (the shop front stays the only entrance)
     - a wide opening in the party wall
     - roof
   - Changed tiles stream as TILE_PATCH. Placement, paint and seats work there because they already key off the interior/owned zones.
   - Other listings are news only: you can read them on the board but can't buy them (a second branch is BACKLOG).
6. **Notice board and gossip.**
   - A notice board stands at the junction corner. It's a new site you can visit like a shop and appears on the map.
   - Its panel lists current listings: price, days left, and buy or rent buttons on the two spaces that join your shop.
   - The sim keeps a short **news log**: listed, sold, closed, a resident moved in or out, a low price day at the market.
   - **Who tells it.** A resident you serve who hasn't heard a newer item tells you one. The UI shows their name and a line for a few seconds, and it's added to a "news" list on the evening panel.
   - **First regulars.** The first two regulars each morning bring the overnight news, as DESIGN's core loop describes.
7. **Offline catch-up.**
   - Each missed day (1–7, by the existing rule) runs `Game.abstractDay()`. Sleep uses the same daily world step, so catch-up matches live play.
   - **The daily world step:**
     - market refresh
     - property market roll
     - resident life events
     - news
     - every pending delivery arrives
   - **With staff hired and paid:**
     - The shop opens in the abstract day: cups = the usual day's arrivals × appeal × `catchup.json` staff factor, capped by the ingredients in stock.
     - Each cup is paid at the auto-serve rate of 70 %, with no tips.
     - Stock never goes below zero, and wages and rent are paid as at Sleep.
   - **Without staff** the shop stays closed, and the world still moves on.
   - **Afterwards** a "while you were away" panel shows: days, cups and income, wages, news, and listings that opened or closed.
8. **Vehicles are decoration.**
   - Cars, trishaws and bicycles drive the road lanes. They're simulated in `render/` only, with seeded cosmetic randomness, and never touch sim state.
   - They stop before a zebra crossing while anyone mirrored is on it.
   - There are at most 6 at once; Low tier gets 3.
   - They use pooled sorted sprites and new placeholder art.
9. **Pavement separation** (the BACKLOG item). Walkers on the pavement pick a lane row from their handle, so two people heading the same way don't overlap in single file. It's a per-walker lane offset applied to the goal row, and nothing changes in pathfinding.
10. **Not in M4** (stays where DESIGN puts it, or goes to BACKLOG):
    - regulars' story arcs and friendship beats, seasons and weather effects (M5)
    - a broker (pwèza)
    - a second branch on another street
    - buying houses to live in or rent out
    - real shop interiors
    - courier NPCs
    - an upstairs room or rooftop

## S2: Residents and schedules (sim)

- `src/data/residents.json` and `shared/residents.ts` (a validating builder, the `buildStaff` pattern).
- Places are derived from the neighbourhood data: doors, stall spots, street ends, the shop front.
- `sim/npc/residents.ts` keeps residents as SoA:
  - position, path, state (inside / walking / at stall / shopping)
  - current block, home, look, visits, `moved`
- **Hourly decision step.** A resident inside a place is hidden. Walking reuses `sim/npc/walker.ts` with the `people` access class.
- **Tea shop block.** It hands over to `Customers.spawnResident(r)`, which walks from their door. When the visit ends they walk home instead of to a random entry.
- A path cache in `sim/path/` keyed by (from, to, class, grid version).
- **Tests:**
  - everyone is home at night
  - schedules are deterministic for a seed
  - resident visits are counted
  - generic plus resident arrivals stay within ±10 % of the M3 daily customer count
  - no resident ever stands on road or filler
  - path budget within 700 nodes per tick

## S3: LOD streaming and render (protocol v11)

- **`viewport` action.** `render/camera.ts` sends it when the view moves more than 2 tiles, throttled.
- **Near set.** The worker keeps a near-flag per NPC (residents, customers, workers): spawn and despawn on entering or leaving the near set, and STATE only for near NPCs.
- **Main mirror** releases sprites on despawn, as now.
- **Render:**
  - New def ids `NPC_DEF_RESIDENT` and `NPC_DEF_KEEPER`.
  - Keepers become real NPCs; `render/street-shops.ts` stops drawing static keepers.
  - Resident looks reuse the recolour pipeline.
- **Tests:**
  - frame traffic is 0 NPC_STATE for NPCs off the viewport
  - moving the viewport spawns exactly the NPCs inside it
  - the sim outcome is identical with two different viewports (determinism)

## S6: Notice board, buying and renting, growing the shop

- **The board as a site.** The notice board site goes in `neighbourhood.json`, with a sprite, the `visit` task and a map button.
- **Panel.** `ui/board/panel.ts` lists the listings and has buy and rent buttons.
- **Actions** `buyProperty {id}` and `rentProperty {id}` check that:
  - the player is at the board
  - the space is listed
  - there's enough money

  Rent is paid at Sleep, and the lease ends calmly when it can't be paid.
- **Growing the area.** `sim/area.ts` builds expansion spaces from the data:
  - zones
  - floor
  - sills
  - the party-wall opening
  - roof

  `Game` diffs the new area against the current one and streams patches for the changed rect. It also updates walk and property-grid bases there.
- **Placement and paint** use the owned zone rather than `SHOP` rects. Check `checkPaint` and `wayStaysOpen`.
- **Lease end.** Objects in the space go to the inventory (counted as `returned`), and the tiles go back to the facade.
- **Tests:**
  - buying works only while listed and at the board, and money never goes negative
  - seats in the new space are chosen by customers
  - paint works there
  - a lease ends with no debt and nothing lost
  - an expanded shop survives a save round trip

## Changes found while building

- **No path cache yet.** Residents add about one path request every few seconds, well inside the 700-node budget; the cache waits until the gate shows a need (S11).
- **Keepers aren't NPCs while at work.** The keeper tile is behind the counter (blocked), and NPCs there sort badly against the stall sprites. A keeper walks to the stall's visit tile and "goes in"; the stall then draws its keeper sprite (`PlayerState.keepers`, a bit per shop). Before opening and after closing the stall stands empty.
- **The property roll uses its own stream per (seed, day)** instead of the shared `events` stream, so a caught-up day rolls exactly like a played one whatever else drew from `events` (the nightly teaser does).
- **The save code grew** from ~800 to ~1,900 characters (properties and residents, ~0.9 KB). It is the full backup; share links (M6) get their own compressed layout-only format for DESIGN's 2,000-character target. The size test now allows 2,500.
- **Renting charges nothing up front**: rent is paid at each Sleep, the first one that night.
- **Buying a lot also clears the party wall**: wall items hanging where the wall opens go back to the inventory (the toast says so).
- **Resident plans use their own rng per (seed, day, resident)**, like the market's daily stock, so a reload mid-day gives the same plan and one resident never shifts another's.
- `my.json` had `build.err.noWall` twice (an M1 wall-decor line and the M3 paint line); JSON keeps the last one, which is the one in use. Rewriting the file kept that one.
